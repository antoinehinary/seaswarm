the person sooner than the fixed lawnmower baseline: within 60 minutes greedy finds 18 to 36

to find. Greedy and the bundle auction are statistically tied (paired differences
within ±8 points, every interval includes zero): with replanning every two minutes, looking three
tiles ahead adds little over taking the best tile each time. Fleet composition matters as much as
the algorithm: USVs sweep the most area per hour and are the only vehicles that can recover the
person, EO drones lose most of their value at night, and larger fleets need more coordination
(up to 29 separation refusals per run), all resolved by the safety layer without a single
violation in 600 runs.
